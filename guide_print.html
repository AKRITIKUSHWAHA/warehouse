3 Step-by-Step Practical Walkthrough

Step 1: Inbound Receiving at Miami CFS Dock Menu: Warehouse Receipts
When to use: Whenever a delivery driver drops off packages at your Miami warehouse facility.
  1. Click + New Receipt on the Warehouse Receipts screen.
  2. Select the US Shipper and the Caribbean Consignee from your central customer directory.
  3. Enter pieces, gross weight (lbs), dimensions (L × W × H inches), and assign warehouse rack storage.
  4. Click Save to generate a unique Receipt Number (e.g., WR-2026-0045).
✓ Result: Printable barcode label generated. Customer receives intake confirmation and their House Bill of Lading (HBL) is automatically staged.
Step 2: Ocean Container Consolidation & Stuffing Menu: Container Consolidation
When to use: When packing a 20ft or 40ft ocean container for a specific Caribbean destination port.
  1. Click + New Consolidation and choose the destination port (e.g., Road Town Port, Tortola).
  2. Enter the carrier container number (e.g., TGHU1234567) and the heavy-duty bolt seal number.
  3. Select the pending warehouse receipts to pack into this container. Total pieces, weight, and volume (CBM) calculate instantly.
  4. Click Confirm Consolidation to generate your unique Consolidation Number (e.g., CNS-2026-0082).
✓ Result: All individual customer packages are locked into this specific ocean container with official seal tracking.
Step 3: Issuing the Master Bill of Lading (MBL) Menu: Master Bills of Lading
When to use: When container packing is finished and cargo is booked on the ocean feeder ship.
  1. Click + Issue Master B/L.
  2. Step 1 Dropdown: Select your Consolidation Number. The system instantly auto-populates the container number, bolt seal, destination port, and all linked House B/Ls!
  3. Select the Feeder Vessel from your Fleet Directory (e.g. M/V Island Trader).
  4. Review total gross weight and CBM, then click Issue Master B/L.
✓ Result: Your legal ocean carriage contract is issued. Container status automatically updates to "In Transit".
Step 4: 1-Click Customs Cargo Manifest Menu: Customs Manifests
When to use: Immediately after Master B/L issuance, prior to feeder vessel departure.
  1. Go to the Manifests dashboard and click Generate Manifest.
  2. Select the active Master B/L.
  3. The system automatically aggregates every package, description, piece count, and consignee into the official customs export layout.
  4. Download the formatted PDF or transmit electronically to island customs authorities.
✓ Result: 100% automated documentation. Zero manual re-entry errors. Destination customs begins pre-clearance while the ship is sailing.
Step 5: Island Arrival, Deconsolidation & Final Delivery Menu: Agent Portal (/agent/shipments)
When to use: When the feeder ship arrives at the destination port (Tortola, St. Kitts) and the container is discharged.
  1. The destination island agent receives the container at their local CFS and opens it for deconsolidation.
  2. Packages are checked against the manifest and local customs tariffs/landing fees are verified.
  3. When the customer arrives for collection (or upon scheduled door delivery), the agent clicks Mark Delivered / Release Cargo.
  4. Customer signature and delivery timestamp are captured in the system.
✓ Result: Shipment lifecycle is marked DELIVERED & COMPLETED. Proof of Delivery (POD) permanently archived.
